Our Story

Started from a real problem

Resolvo started as an internal support tool for Indian D2C brands. Over time, we noticed that the same core problems — missed client communications, double-bookings, no follow-up system — affected almost every service professional in India, not just ecommerce brands.

In 2025 we expanded Resolvo to serve any appointment-driven professional. The free plan for service professionals is our commitment to making modern practice management accessible to everyone in India, regardless of the size of their practice.
