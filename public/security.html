Security

How we protect your support data.

Customer conversations are sensitive. This page says what we do, in plain words, and what we have not done yet.

Where your data lives

Plain facts about the servers.

  • Each brand has its own database. One brand's tickets and customers are never stored in another brand's database.
  • Resolvo runs on a dedicated server from Contabo in a data centre in the European Union.
  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • Access tokens for WhatsApp (Meta) and social channels are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).

Who can get in

Sign-in and permissions.

  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes (bcrypt). We cannot read them.
  • Too many wrong passwords and sign-in is paused for 15 minutes.
  • Optional two-factor sign-in on Pro and above.
  • On the phone, an app lock with PIN, fingerprint or face. The PIN is kept only on your phone, as a salted hash, and too many wrong tries sign you out.
  • Admins decide who can add people and change settings. Sessions expire (8 hours, or 30 days if you choose to stay signed in).
  • When our team opens your workspace to help you, it is written to a log.

Backups and recovery

So a mistake or a failure is never the end.

  • Every night, every workspace and all settings are backed up and the backup is checked straight after. If a backup fails, we are alerted.
  • We keep the last 14 days, then one a week for 8 weeks.
  • Anything our team deletes (a workspace, a user, a ticket) goes into a 30-day recycle bin first, so it can be restored.

A record of changes

Know who did what.

  • The audit trail records users added, roles and status changed, password changes and imports, with who did them and when.
  • Ticket activity is recorded on each ticket.

Your data and your rights

You stay in charge of your customers' data.

  • You own the data you put into Resolvo. We use it only to run your helpdesk. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Export your data as CSV whenever you like.
  • Ask us to delete your data at contact@resolvogroup.com. Account data is deleted within 30 days of a closure request. Copies in our nightly backups age out within about 10 weeks.
  • The Enterprise plan adds erasure and retention tools for answering your own customers' requests.
  • Read the full privacy policy.

Found a security problem?

We want to hear about it first.

Email contact@resolvogroup.com with the word Security in the subject. Tell us what you found and how to reproduce it. We reply within one working day. Please give us a reasonable time to fix a problem before you share it publicly.

What we have not done yet

We would rather be honest than impressive.

Resolvo has not completed a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 audit. We are a new product, and we will say so here when that changes. If your company needs a security questionnaire answered, send it to us and we will fill it in honestly.
