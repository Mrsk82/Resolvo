7 Appointment Reminder Email Templates That Actually Work

A well-timed reminder email cuts no-shows by 40–60%. Here are seven copy-paste templates for clinics, law firms, salons, tutors, and consultants — personalised for each profession.

By Resolvo Team · 4 min read · June 2025

What makes a reminder email actually work

Bad reminder emails get ignored. Good ones get read and acted on. The difference comes down to three things: timing, specificity, and a clear action the client can take if they need to reschedule.

  • Timing — 24 hours before is the sweet spot. Far enough to reschedule, close enough to remember.
  • Specificity — include the exact date, time, location, and what the client should bring or prepare.
  • Easy out — a one-click reschedule link converts no-shows into rescheduled appointments instead of lost revenue.

Template 1: General appointment reminder

Subject: Reminder — Your appointment is tomorrow at [TIME]

Hi [Client Name], Just a quick reminder that you have an appointment tomorrow: 📅 Date: [DATE] ⏰ Time: [TIME] 📍 Location: [ADDRESS or "Online via Google Meet"] If you need to reschedule, click here: [RESCHEDULE LINK] See you tomorrow, [Your Name / Practice Name]

Template 2: Doctor / Clinic reminder

Subject: Appointment reminder — [Clinic Name], tomorrow at [TIME]

Hi [Patient Name], This is a reminder for your appointment at [Clinic Name]: 📅 Date: [DATE] ⏰ Time: [TIME] 🩺 Doctor: Dr. [Name] Please bring: • Any previous reports or prescriptions • Your Aadhar card / insurance card (if applicable) • Arrive 5 minutes early for paperwork Need to reschedule? Click here: [LINK] Take care, [Clinic Name] [Phone Number]

Template 3: Salon / Spa reminder

Subject: See you tomorrow at [TIME]! ✂️

Hi [Client Name], Your appointment at [Salon Name] is confirmed for tomorrow! 📅 [DATE] at [TIME] 💇 Service: [SERVICE NAME] 👩 Stylist: [STYLIST NAME] A few things: • Please arrive 5 minutes early • Let us know if you've changed your hair since your last visit • Parking available at [PARKING DETAILS] Need to reschedule? No problem: [LINK] See you soon! Team [Salon Name]

Template 4: Lawyer / Advocate reminder

Template 5: Tutor / Coach reminder

Subject: Session reminder — tomorrow at [TIME]

Hi [Student/Parent Name], Reminder for tomorrow's session: 📅 [DATE] at [TIME] 📚 Subject: [SUBJECT] 📍 [Location or "Online — I'll send the meeting link 30 mins before"] Please make sure [Student Name]: • Has completed any homework from the last session • Has the textbook / notes ready • Joins 2 minutes early if it's an online session Need to reschedule? Let me know here: [LINK] See you tomorrow, [Tutor Name]
