Where to Share Your Booking Link to Get More Appointments

Creating a booking link is step one. Getting appointments from it requires putting it in the right places — not just on your website. Here are the 8 highest-leverage places to share your booking link, and exactly what to say in each one.

By Resolvo Team · 5 min read · June 2025

1. WhatsApp auto-reply

The highest-impact placement for most Indian service businesses. When someone messages you on WhatsApp, they see your auto-reply before they see you. Set it to:

"Hi! Thanks for reaching out to [Business Name]. To book an appointment, use our online booking link: [URL]. For other questions, we'll reply shortly."

This converts "can I book?" WhatsApp messages into self-service bookings without any manual back-and-forth.

2. WhatsApp Business profile

Your WhatsApp Business profile has a website field and a description. Put the booking link in the website field and mention it in the description: "Book appointments online: [URL]." People who look at your profile before messaging will often book directly.

3. Instagram bio

Instagram's bio link is one of the most underused real estate for service businesses. Replace any generic website link with your direct booking link. The copy: "Book a [service] appointment →" works significantly better than "Book now" or "Visit website" because it's specific about what the click will do.

Use a link-in-bio tool (like Linktree) only if you need to share multiple links; otherwise, go directly to your booking page for the best conversion.

4. Google Business Profile (Google Maps)

If you have a Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business), add your booking link in the "Appointments" URL field. When people find you on Google Maps and tap "Book an appointment," they go directly to your booking page. This is high-intent traffic — people searching for your service type in your area.

💡 Google Business Profile is the single highest-ROI place for local service businesses. People searching "dentist near me" or "salon in Koramangala" are ready to book. A booking link in your Business Profile captures them at peak intent.

5. Email signature

Every email you send is a potential appointment. Add a one-line CTA to your email signature:

"Book an appointment: [your-booking-link.com]"

It takes 2 minutes to set up and works passively forever. Especially effective for consultants and professionals who email clients regularly.

6. Post-appointment reminder messages

When you send an appointment confirmation or reminder via WhatsApp or SMS, include a rescheduling link at the bottom: "Need to reschedule? Use this link: [URL]." This removes the friction of having to call, and it captures rescheduling intent immediately instead of having clients forget or not bother.

7. In your physical space (QR code)

A QR code that links to your booking page, placed in the right spots, converts foot traffic into future bookings:

  • At the reception desk: "Book your next appointment now before you leave"
  • On the invoice or bill: "Book again here →"
  • On a table tent in waiting areas: "Save time next visit — book online"

Generate a free QR code for your booking link at any QR code generator and print it on a laminated card or sticker.

8. Follow-up message after service

Send every client a follow-up message 2–3 days after their appointment. Most businesses send nothing. A simple "Hope you're happy with your [service] yesterday. Ready to book your next visit?" with the booking link converts a significant number of one-time clients into repeat ones.
