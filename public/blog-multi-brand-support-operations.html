Running Support for Multiple Brands from One Dashboard

If you run two businesses, manage support for multiple clients, or operate under different brand names, you know the chaos: separate inboxes, separate logins, no visibility across them. Here's how to consolidate everything into one operation without mixing up customers.

By Resolvo Team · 6 min read · June 2025

Who needs multi-brand support

Multi-brand support operations come in several forms:

  • D2C businesses with multiple product lines — a company that sells skincare under one brand and supplements under another, each with its own support email
  • BPO / outsourced support — a support team that handles customer service for 3–5 client companies
  • Founders with multiple ventures — different SaaS products or service businesses that share a small team
  • Franchise operations — central support that handles queries for multiple locations under different regional identities

In all these cases, the core problem is the same: multiple inboxes, multiple identities, one team trying to keep them all organised.

The multi-inbox problem

The typical multi-brand setup: each brand has its own Gmail, each agent logs in to each account separately. Agent A checks Brand 1's Gmail, then Brand 2's Gmail, then Brand 3's. They switch between tabs. They accidentally reply to Brand 2's customer from Brand 1's account. They lose track of which brand has SLA breaches. Nobody has visibility across all brands simultaneously.

This scales terribly. By the time you have 3 brands and 4 agents, coordination overhead is eating 2–3 hours of each agent's day.

The unified inbox model

The solution is a single helpdesk that connects all brand inboxes. In Resolvo:

  1. Each brand is a separate workspace with its own email address, templates, and settings
  2. All tickets from all brands appear in a single dashboard, clearly labelled by brand
  3. Agents can filter to see only Brand 1's tickets, or see all brands together
  4. Replies go out from the correct brand email automatically — no accidental cross-brand replies
  5. SLA monitoring runs per brand, so you can see breach status across all brands at once

💡 Template separation is critical. Each brand needs its own reply templates with the right name, tone, and sign-off. A customer of your premium skincare brand should not receive a reply signed with your budget supplement brand's name. Resolvo keeps templates per brand, so agents always pull from the right set.

Staffing multi-brand support

Two models work well depending on volume:

Generalist model (low volume)

All agents handle all brands. Good when total volume is under 50 tickets/day. Agents need to know all brands' products and policies, but the flexibility allows any agent to pick up any ticket. Works well when brands are related (e.g., different product lines from the same company).

Specialist model (higher volume)

Each agent is assigned to specific brands. Agent A handles Brand 1 and Brand 2; Agent B handles Brand 3. Better when brands are very different (different industries, different customer types) or when volume is high enough to justify specialisation. Requires more headcount but produces better quality responses.

⚠️ Avoid the hybrid trap. "Some agents do all brands sometimes" — this is the worst of both worlds. Agents lose familiarity with each brand's specifics, mistakes increase, and accountability is unclear. Pick one model and stick to it.

Reporting across multiple brands

One of the biggest benefits of unified multi-brand support is comparative reporting. You can see:

  • Which brand is generating the most tickets? (Might indicate a product or communication problem)
  • Which brand has the worst CSAT? (Might indicate the team handling it needs coaching)
  • Which brand has the longest average resolution time? (Might indicate process or product complexity)

This visibility is impossible when brands run in separate Gmail accounts with no consolidated data.
