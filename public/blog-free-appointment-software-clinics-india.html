Best Free Appointment Software for Clinics in India (2025)

Managing patient appointments over phone calls costs your clinic 2–3 hours every day and leads to double-bookings, no-shows, and frustrated patients. Here's how to fix that — for free.

By Resolvo Team · 6 min read · June 2025

The real cost of phone-based appointment management

A busy clinic in India typically handles 30–60 appointments per day. If each booking takes 3–5 minutes on the phone — confirming availability, spelling out the patient's name, sending a WhatsApp reminder — that's nearly 3 hours of receptionist time per day, just on scheduling.

That doesn't include the missed calls (patients who called during a busy period and booked elsewhere), the double-bookings (two patients given the same slot), or the no-shows (patients who forgot and nobody followed up).

Online appointment software eliminates all three problems. Patients book when it suits them — 11pm on a Sunday if they want — and they get an automatic confirmation and reminder without anyone picking up a phone.

What to look for in clinic appointment software

  • Public booking link — patients should be able to book without creating an account or downloading an app
  • Auto-confirmation emails — sent the moment a booking is made
  • Reminder emails or SMS — sent 24 hours before the appointment
  • Slot management — define your working hours and slot duration (15 min, 30 min, 1 hour)
  • Patient history — see what the patient booked before, any notes from previous visits
  • No patient login required — friction kills bookings

💡 Key insight: The best appointment tool for a small clinic is not the most feature-rich one — it's the one your patients will actually use. A simple link they can tap on WhatsApp wins over a complex portal every time.

2. Practo

Practo has a large patient base searching for doctors, so it drives discovery. However, you're listed alongside competitors, and Practo takes a cut for premium placements. For an established clinic that wants an independent booking system, it's not the right fit.

3. Calendly

Calendly is excellent for one-on-one scheduling but lacks patient history, clinic-specific workflows, and support ticketing. The free plan limits you to one event type, which doesn't work well for multi-type consultations.

4. SimplyBook.me

Good for salons and spas. The free plan is limited to 50 bookings per month — too low for most clinics. Paid plans start at $9.9/month.

How to reduce no-shows with online booking

No-shows are a ₹500–₹2,000 loss per missed slot (opportunity cost). Here's how online booking reduces them:

  • Confirmation email — sent immediately after booking, with the date, time, and a calendar invite link
  • Reminder email — sent automatically 24 hours before the appointment
  • Easy cancellation — patients can cancel via the confirmation email link, freeing the slot for someone else

Clinics using online booking with automatic reminders report 40–60% fewer no-shows compared to phone-only scheduling.

What about patients who prefer phone calls?

Keep your phone line. Online booking is for patients who prefer it (increasingly, everyone under 40). You'll handle phone bookings the same way you always have — but fewer of them, because many patients will self-serve online.

Over time, as word spreads that your clinic has an online booking link, the ratio shifts. Most clinics see 50–70% of new bookings come through the online link within 3 months of going live.
