Features

Everything your support team needs, in one calm inbox.

Resolvo is a helpdesk for small teams in India. Here is what it does today, in plain words, and what you need for each part.

One inbox for every conversation

Email, WhatsApp and your website chat land in one queue, sorted by who has waited longest.

Shared inbox with Focus mode

A calm queue that shows what needs you now. Open a ticket, answer it, move to the next. Overdue and Due soon are one tap away.

Email ticketing Pro

Connect the Gmail or support address you already use. Customer emails become tickets with an owner, a priority and an SLA clock.

WhatsApp Business

Reply to WhatsApp customers from the same inbox, through the official WhatsApp Business API (Twilio or Meta Cloud API).

Website chat widget Enterprise

Add one line to your site and visitors can write to you. Their messages arrive as tickets.

Instagram, Facebook and Telegram

Messages and comments from these channels can arrive in the same inbox. Meta channels need your Meta business verification and app review.

Customer self-service portal Enterprise

Customers log in to see their tickets and answers without emailing you.

Answer faster, with less typing

Everything that saves a minute on a reply, when you write a hundred a day.

AI reply drafts Pro

Open a ticket and get a draft in your brand's tone. Your agent edits and sends. Nothing is ever sent without a person clicking send.

Saved replies and replies for the moment

Order status, refund, delay, follow-up and thanks. Pick one and the customer's name, order number and your name are filled in.

Smart queue assignment Pro

New tickets go to the right person, so agents stop stepping on each other.

Internal notes, snooze and bulk actions

Leave notes only your team sees, snooze a ticket until it matters again, and act on many tickets at once.

Automatic acknowledgements

Customers get an instant "we have your message" email. It is marked as an automatic reply so it never starts an email loop.

Knowledge base Pro

Write help articles once and reuse them in replies and for your customers.

Keep your promises

Know which customer is about to wait too long, before they ask.

SLA clocks and alerts

Every ticket has a target time. Overdue and Due soon tickets are counted on your Today cards and can buzz your phone.

Escalation rules

When a ticket sits too long or is marked critical, the right people hear about it.

CSAT and NPS

Ask customers how it went and see the scores next to your ticket numbers.

Audit trail Free

A record of who changed what: tickets, users, roles and passwords.

Know who you are talking to

A customer record that shows the whole story next to the ticket.

Customer record

Every email, WhatsApp number and ticket of one person in one place, with badges for VIP, at risk and happy.

Shopify orders next to the ticket

See the customer's order, its status and tracking number beside the conversation. Read-only: Resolvo never changes your store. One tap writes a reply from the order's real status.

Duplicate customers

When the same person writes from email and WhatsApp, Resolvo offers to join the two records.

Sales pipeline

Track deals for the customers who are also leads, without another tool.

See how the team is doing

Plain answers instead of a wall of charts.

Insights Pro

An overview, plain-language questions ("who is slowest this week?") and a team scorecard.

SLA, workload and response-time reports Pro

Where tickets wait, who is overloaded and how fast you answer first.

Multiple brands, one dashboard

Give each brand its own inbox, portal and SLA policy and watch all of them from one place.

Built for the phone

Most support happens away from a desk. The Android app and the website work the same way.

Swipe to take, resolve or snooze

Swipe a ticket right to take it and all the way to resolve it, with an Undo.

Write with no signal

Replies and new tickets written offline are saved and sent by themselves when you are back online. Unsent drafts are kept.

Dictate, photos and files

Speak your reply in English, Hindi and other Indian languages, or add a photo from the camera.

App lock and widget

Lock the app with a PIN, fingerprint or face (Android app). Add a home-screen widget with your open and overdue tickets.

Alerts with buttons

Alerts about a ticket come with Reply, Assign to me and Resolve (Chrome on Android and installed web app).

Android app and iPhone

Download the Android app, or open Resolvo in Safari on iPhone and add it to the home screen. Get the app.

Run it safely

Controls and safety nets for the person in charge.

Roles and permissions

Admins decide who can add people and change settings.

Two-factor sign-in Pro

A second step keeps an account safe if a password leaks.

Nightly backups

Every workspace is backed up every night and the backup is checked. How we protect your data.

Data export and erasure tools Enterprise

Export your data, and use the erasure and retention tools when a customer asks.
