Before you submit a template
    

  • A message you start yourself, outside the 24 hours after a customer last wrote, must use a template that WhatsApp has approved. Inside those 24 hours you can write freely.
    • 

  • Order updates belong in the Utility category. If a Utility template reads like a promotion, WhatsApp can move it to Marketing, which is priced differently.
    • 

  • Keep variables in order ({{1}}, {{2}}...), give an example for each, and keep the text short and plain.
    • 

  • You submit templates in Meta's WhatsApp Manager, or in your Twilio console if you use Twilio. Approval can take minutes or a day. WhatsApp's rules change, so read their current guidance before you submit.
    • 

  • Resolvo answers customer messages in the shared inbox and tells you when the 24-hour window has closed. How WhatsApp works in Resolvo.
