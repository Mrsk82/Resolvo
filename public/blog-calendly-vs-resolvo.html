Calendly vs Resolvo: Which Booking Tool Is Better for Indian Businesses?

Calendly is the world's most popular scheduling tool. But it's priced in dollars, built for Western sales teams, and missing features that Indian service professionals actually need. Here's an honest comparison.

By Resolvo Team · 8 min read · June 2025

Who is each tool built for?

Calendly was built for B2B sales teams in the US — primarily for scheduling sales calls, demos, and interview slots. It's excellent at that specific use case. The product assumes you're connecting to a Google Calendar or Outlook, scheduling 30-minute video calls, and integrating with a CRM like Salesforce.

Resolvo was built for Indian service professionals — doctors, lawyers, consultants, salons, tutors — who need a booking page that works on mobile, sends WhatsApp-friendly confirmation emails, and doesn't require a credit card to start. It bundles scheduling with customer support, issue tracking, and CRM in one platform.

Pricing comparison

⚠️ Calendly's free tier is very limited: 1 event type, no team features, no custom branding removal, no analytics. To get useful features you need the Standard plan at $10/user/month — that's ₹840/month at current exchange rates, per user.

Where Calendly is better

  • Calendar integrations — Calendly connects to Google Calendar, Outlook, iCloud, and automatically blocks times when you're busy. Resolvo uses its own schedule configuration.
  • Video call links — Calendly auto-generates Zoom/Meet/Teams links for each booking. Useful for remote consultations.
  • Routing forms — For sales teams that need to qualify leads before booking, Calendly's routing forms are powerful.
  • Brand recognition — Clients in corporate India may be more familiar with Calendly links.

  • Everything in one platform — Customer emails, bug tracking, CRM, and appointments in the same system. No integrations needed.

Who should use Calendly?

Use Calendly if:

  • You're a B2B salesperson scheduling video calls
  • You rely heavily on Google Calendar sync (your availability comes from an existing calendar)
  • You need Zoom/Meet links auto-generated per booking
  • Your clients are international and expect a Calendly link

Who should use Resolvo?

Use Resolvo if:

  • You're a doctor, lawyer, consultant, salon, or tutor in India
  • You want scheduling + customer support + notes in one tool
  • You're seeing under 250 patients/clients/students per month (free)
  • You don't want to pay in dollars
  • You want a booking page with your own branding from day one
