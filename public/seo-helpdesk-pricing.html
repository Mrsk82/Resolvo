The short answer

Most helpdesks charge per agent, per month, usually in US dollars. That is fine for one person and gets expensive as the team grows. Resolvo charges per brand instead, so the price does not move when you add a teammate.

Figures below are list prices as we last checked them in September 2026: annual billing, before tax, per agent per month. Vendors change prices and plans often, so please confirm on their pricing pages before you decide.

What is not in those numbers

  • WhatsApp. On Freshdesk it needs Omni, roughly $29 to $119 per agent a month on top. On Zendesk it needs a Suite plan or an add-on.
  • AI. Freshdesk's Freddy AI Copilot is a further $29 per agent a month. Zendesk's AI is priced separately.
  • Jira Service Management has a free tier capped at 3 agents, and its paid Standard plan is around $20 to $24 per agent a month.

Resolvo's AI reply drafts and triage are part of Pro. A person always approves a draft before it is sent.

Eight questions to ask any helpdesk vendor

  1. Do you charge per agent, and what happens to the price when I add three people?
  2. Is WhatsApp included, or a separate add-on?
  3. Is AI included, or charged per agent or per resolution?
  4. Can I pay in rupees and get a proper GST invoice?
  5. What is the price after the free trial, and does it renew automatically?
  6. Which plan has reply deadlines and reports?
  7. Can I export my data if I leave?
  8. How long does setup take, and do I need a consultant?

Pay attention to the answers about add-ons. The base price on a pricing page is rarely the number on the invoice.

Where Resolvo is not the right choice

Resolvo is built for teams of about 2 to 10 who handle support by email and WhatsApp. If you need phone support, a marketing campaign tool or a large enterprise process, a bigger helpdesk will serve you better today. We would rather say so than have you find out later.
