What Resolvo connects to

Set up each one from Settings in a few minutes. Plan tags show where a feature starts.

Gmail and other email Pro

Connect your support address (Gmail, Google Workspace or any IMAP mailbox). Customer emails become tickets and your replies go out from your own address.

WhatsApp Business

Use the official WhatsApp Business API. Connect through Twilio or directly through Meta Cloud API. You need a verified WhatsApp Business number. Replies follow WhatsApp's 24-hour window and Resolvo tells you when it has closed.

Shopify

See the customer's orders, status and tracking inside the ticket and on the customer profile. Connect with your store address and a Client ID and secret (or an access token). Resolvo only reads orders, it never changes them.

Google Calendar

Show and book appointments against your calendar.

Slack alerts

Send new and overdue ticket alerts to a Slack channel with a webhook URL.

Website chat widget Enterprise

Put a chat button on your site with one line of code. Messages arrive as tickets.

Instagram, Facebook Pages and Telegram

Bring messages and comments into the inbox. Instagram and Facebook need a verified Meta business and Meta's app review before real customers can use them.

Alerts on your phone

Browser and app alerts for new, assigned and overdue tickets. In Chrome on Android they carry Reply, Assign and Resolve buttons.

Zapier and your own tools Enterprise

Create tickets from a web form, Zapier or your own system through the ticket API (see below).

Website chat widget

Replace YOUR-WORKSPACE with your workspace name (its short address in Resolvo) and paste this before the closing body tag of your site.

<script src="https://app.resolvogroup.com/widget/YOUR-WORKSPACE/widget.js"></script>

Create tickets from your own tools

One call creates a ticket. Use it from a form, a script or Zapier's webhook step.

curl -X POST https://app.resolvogroup.com/api/tickets/public/create \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "x-api-token: YOUR_API_TOKEN" \
  -d '{"subject":"Refund not received","email":"customer@example.com","name":"Asha","body":"I returned the order 7 days ago.","priority":"High"}'

The answer is {"success":true,"ticketId":"TKT-..."}. priority can be Critical, High, Medium or Low. Create an API token in Settings, then API.
