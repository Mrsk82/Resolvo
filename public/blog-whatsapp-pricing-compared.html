WhatsApp Support Pricing Compared: Freshdesk, Zendesk, and What "Included" Actually Means

If your customers reach you on WhatsApp, the sticker price of a helpdesk is rarely what you actually pay. Freshdesk, Zendesk, and Jira Service Management — the three tools small support teams compare most — all treat WhatsApp as something you add on, not something that comes with the base plan. Here's exactly what "included" means at each one, priced out.

By Resolvo Team · 6 min read · September 2026

Freshdesk — Growth plan doesn't include it

Freshdesk's Growth plan runs $19/agent/month billed annually. WhatsApp isn't part of it at any price point — you need Freshdesk Omni (Omnichannel), priced separately at $29-119/agent/month, stacked on top of your base plan. That puts a realistic WhatsApp-enabled Freshdesk seat somewhere between roughly $48 and $138/agent/month, depending on which Omni tier you need — before factoring in Freddy AI Copilot, a further +$29/agent/month if you also want AI features.

Zendesk — WhatsApp lives behind Suite plans

Zendesk's bare Support-only plan is $19/agent/month, but it doesn't include WhatsApp. WhatsApp is gated behind Suite plans or a Social Messaging add-on, plus a per-resolution AI cost on top of that. Suite Team runs $55/agent/month billed annually, and Suite Growth (around $89/month) is the tier usually recommended for a small business — meaning the realistic entry price for WhatsApp on Zendesk starts closer to the Suite tier, not the $19 headline number.

Jira Service Management — no native WhatsApp channel at all

Jira Service Management doesn't have a native WhatsApp channel — this research pass found nothing shipped directly by Atlassian for it. Teams using JSM who need WhatsApp support are relying on third-party connectors or workarounds rather than a first-party feature. What "included" should actually mean

Three different patterns show up across the market, and none of them is what a small team probably means when they hear "WhatsApp support":

  • Bolt-on product you buy separately — Freshdesk's approach, where Omni is its own SKU with its own pricing.
  • Gated behind a higher subscription tier, plus a variable cost — Zendesk's approach, where you need Suite (not the base plan) and pay per AI resolution on top.
  • Not offered at all — Jira Service Management's approach, where you go find and maintain your own integration.
