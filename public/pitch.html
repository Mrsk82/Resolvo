The problem

This is what broken support looks like

Every fast-growing Indian brand hits the same wall. Support becomes the bottleneck.

01
Emails fall through the cracks
Customer emails hit a shared Gmail inbox. No assignment. No tracking. Three agents reply to the same ticket or nobody does. Customers follow up. Trust breaks.
02
WhatsApp is not a support system
Your team is managing 200 customer conversations in personal WhatsApp numbers. No history. Agent leaves, conversations go with them. No way to measure anything.
03
No visibility on what's happening
How many tickets are open right now? What's your average response time? Which agent is overloaded? Nobody knows. Because there's no system — just inboxes.
Features

Everything your support team needs

One platform. No per-agent pricing. No add-ons you didn't ask for.

Shared ticket inbox
Every email, WhatsApp message, and widget submission becomes a ticket. Assigned, tracked, and resolved — with full conversation history. No email lost.
SLA tracking & alerts
Set response time targets by priority. Critical: 4h. High: 8h. Medium: 24h. Resolvo tracks every ticket against the clock and alerts your team before a breach.
WhatsApp support channel
Connect your WhatsApp Business number. Every message becomes a ticket. Reply from the Resolvo dashboard — no need to open WhatsApp. Full thread history maintained.
NEW
AI-drafted replies
Resolvo drafts a reply for every incoming ticket using your previous responses as context. Agent reviews and sends. First response time drops by 60%.
Customer self-service portal
Branded portal at /portal/your-brand. Customers log in via OTP, see all their tickets, add comments, check status. Cuts "what's the update?" emails by 70%.
Team & SLA analytics
Live dashboard showing open tickets, SLA compliance, agent workload, CSAT scores, and response time trends. Know what's working before your manager asks.
Issue tracker built-in
Link support tickets to internal bugs. Engineering sees which issues are causing the most tickets. When the bug is fixed, the linked customer gets notified automatically.
Appointment booking
Let customers book calls with your support team directly from tickets. Automatic confirmations and 1-hour reminders sent via email. No Calendly needed.
