One-time Google set-up (10 minutes, in your own Google account)
  1. Search Console: open search.google.com/search-console, add the property https://app.resolvogroup.com, verify with the HTML tag method (send me the tag and I will place it), then open Sitemaps and submit sitemap.xml.
  2. Bing: open bing.com/webmasters and import the site from Search Console in one click.
  3. Google Analytics: in your GA4 property, open Admin, Events, and mark sign_up, generate_lead, cta_demo and download_android_app as key events.
  4. Remember: Google Analytics only counts visitors who accepted the cookie choice. The numbers on this page count everyone, without cookies.
