Daily Digest Automation: How to Keep Your Team Aligned Without Endless Meetings

The morning standup exists to answer three questions: what happened yesterday, what's happening today, what's blocked. A daily digest answers all three automatically — before the meeting starts, or instead of it entirely. Here's how to set one up.

By Resolvo Team · 6 min read · June 2025

What a daily digest actually is

A daily digest is an automated summary sent each morning (or evening) that gives your team a snapshot of the previous day's activity. For a support team, it might show: tickets opened, closed, SLA breaches, CSAT scores, and any tickets still pending over 24 hours. For an engineering team: issues closed, issues opened, any critical bugs logged, and sprint burn-down status.

The digest pulls data automatically — nobody writes it. It lands in Slack or email at 8:30am. Everyone reads it before they start work. The morning meeting either becomes much shorter (since everyone has context already) or gets cancelled entirely.

What to include in a support team digest

📊 DAILY SUPPORT DIGEST — Friday 27 June 2025 YESTERDAY: • Tickets opened: 47 (+8 vs Thursday) • Tickets closed: 52 ✅ • Avg first response time: 2.4 hours (target: <4h) ✅ • CSAT: 88% (target: 85%) ✅ • SLA breaches: 3 ⚠️ OPEN RIGHT NOW: • Total open tickets: 31 • Overdue (past SLA): 4 🔴 • Assigned to no agent: 2 ← Action needed TOP ISSUE TYPES YESTERDAY: 1. Login / access issues — 14 tickets 2. Billing questions — 11 tickets 3. Feature not working — 9 tickets AGENTS: • Highest volume: Priya (18 tickets) • Best CSAT: Arjun (94%) • Fastest FRT: Dev (avg 58 min) ATTENTION NEEDED: • Ticket #1847 — open 26 hours, enterprise client, no reply yet

💡 The "Attention Needed" section is the most important part. The whole digest could be summarised as a single number, but specific flagged items require immediate action from a named person. This section turns the digest from information into instructions.

What to include in an engineering team digest

🛠 ENGINEERING DIGEST — Friday 27 June 2025 SPRINT STATUS (Day 6 of 10): • Completed: 14 / 23 points (61%) — on track ✅ • In progress: 5 items • Not started: 4 items ISSUES: • Opened yesterday: 8 • Closed yesterday: 11 • New Critical/High: 2 ⚠️ (see below) CRITICAL ATTENTION: 🔴 BUG-892 — Payment timeout on mobile (High) — unassigned 🔴 BUG-895 — CSV export broken for accounts >500 rows (Medium) — assigned to Sneha DEPLOYMENTS YESTERDAY: • v2.4.1 — deployed 16:30 IST — no rollback events ✅

Setting up the digest: two approaches

Approach 1: Use your helpdesk/issue tracker's built-in reports

Resolvo generates daily email reports automatically — configure the metrics you want, set the delivery time, and choose recipients. No coding required. Takes 5 minutes to set up.

Approach 2: Automated Slack post

If your team lives in Slack, set up a scheduled message that pulls from your helpdesk's API. Resolvo has webhook support — the digest can post directly to a #support-digest or #engineering-digest channel at 8:30am every morning.

Making the digest actually get read

Three things make digests get ignored: too much information, no clear action items, sent at the wrong time.

  • Keep it short — if it takes more than 60 seconds to read, it won't be read
  • Always include flagged items requiring action — the digest should tell someone to do something, not just inform them
  • Send it 15–30 minutes before the team starts work — 8:30am for a 9am start, so people see it before the day begins
