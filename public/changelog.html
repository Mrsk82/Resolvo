What's new

What we shipped, week by week.

Resolvo improves every week. Here is every change that is live, newest first.

8 November 2026

Phone: write offline, dictate, photos, lock, widget

  • Write with no signal. Replies and new tickets written offline are saved and sent by themselves when you are back. Unsent drafts are kept for each ticket.
  • Dictate a reply in English, Hindi and other Indian languages. Photos and files can be added from the camera, shrunk first so they send fast.
  • Replies for the moment (order status, refund, delay, follow-up, thanks) with the customer's name, order number and your name filled in. From a Shopify order, Write the reply drafts a full answer from its real status.
  • App lock with PIN, plus fingerprint or face in the Android app. Home-screen widget with your open, overdue and due-soon tickets.
  • Today cards in the inbox now include Overdue and Due soon. Call and WhatsApp buttons on every customer row on the phone.
  • Alerts about a ticket come with Reply, Assign to me and Resolve buttons (Chrome on Android and the installed web app).
  • Android app 1.2 with fingerprint lock, dictation and the widget. Download.
2 November 2026

Safer deletes

  • Anything removed by our team (a workspace, a user, a ticket or a lead) goes to a 30-day recycle bin first, and can be restored with all its data.
31 October 2026

Backups and clearer emails

  • Nightly backups of every workspace, checked after they are made, kept for 14 days and then weekly for 8 weeks.
  • Password reset works with capital letters and spaces in your address, says plainly when an email could not be sent, and sends from Resolvo when your own sending email is not set up.
  • Welcome emails reach new team members the same way. If one cannot be sent, the admin sees a temporary password to pass on.
  • The audit trail now shows users added, roles and passwords changed and imports, with who did them.
  • Acknowledgement emails are marked as automatic replies so two helpdesks never answer each other in a loop.
29 October 2026

Android app and Shopify orders

  • Resolvo for Android and its download page.
  • Shopify orders next to the ticket and on the customer profile: status, items and tracking number. Read-only.
28 October 2026

Phone gestures

  • Swipe a ticket right to take it and all the way to resolve it, with Undo. Swipe left to snooze. Pull to refresh the lists.
  • A slim reply bar that opens the full composer when you need it, and saved replies as chips above the keyboard.
  • Call, WhatsApp and Email buttons on a customer, and fixes for the login and admin screens on small phones.
27 October 2026

A calmer inbox and a new look

  • New inbox with Focus mode and two panels, a Today page, Insights with plain-language answers, and a Settings page that shows the real status of each section.
  • Customers with health badges (VIP, at risk, happy), last contact and filters. A command palette that finds tickets and customers.
  • Installable app for the phone with alerts on your device.
  • Clear messages when connecting email goes wrong, a WhatsApp setup guide, and no more dead ends for agents.
  • Three new guides and a free support-team calculator.
