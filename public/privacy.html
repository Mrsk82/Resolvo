Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Resolvo ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at app.resolvogroup.com and provides customer support (shared inbox, ticketing, WhatsApp and email) and appointment management services to businesses and professionals in India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our services.

By using Resolvo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create a Resolvo account, we collect your name, email address, company or practice name, and a password.

Client/appointment data: When you or your clients book appointments, we store the client's name, email, phone number, appointment date and time, and any notes provided during booking.

Usage data: We automatically collect information about how you use the service, including log data, IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and actions taken within the app.

Website visit counts: On our public website pages we count page views without cookies and without storing your IP address. Only the page, the source you came from (for example a search engine or a link we shared), and whether you use a phone or a computer are counted, and a short-lived code used to avoid counting one visitor twice is discarded every day. If you send us a sign-up, demo request or message, we also save where you came from (for example the campaign in the link) with that request, so we know which channels bring useful enquiries. This is not applied inside the signed-in app, and a browser that sends the Do Not Track signal is not counted.

Cookies: We use cookies to keep you signed in. Google Analytics cookies are only set if you accept them in the cookie choice on our website, and you can change that choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of a page. You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies, but some parts of the service may not function correctly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To enable appointment booking and send confirmation and reminder emails to your clients
  • To send you service-related notifications (account updates, security alerts)
  • To analyse usage patterns and improve the service
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To send product updates and newsletters (you can opt out at any time)

We do not sell your data or your clients' data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit and access controls to prevent unauthorised access.

Your client data is stored in isolated, per-account databases. No other Resolvo account can access your data.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we will delete your data within 30 days of your request. Appointment and ticket history may be retained for up to 90 days after account closure for legal and audit purposes. 5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may receive some of your data:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis, only if you accept the cookie choice
  • Gmail / Google Workspace and Google Calendar — email and appointments you connect
  • Google Gemini — AI reply drafts, when that feature is on
  • Twilio and Meta — WhatsApp and social messages you connect
  • Shopify — reading orders (read-only), when you connect a store
  • Slack — alerts you choose to send
  • Contabo — server hosting in the European Union

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle your data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Export your data in CSV format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@resolvogroup.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Resolvo is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or by posting a notice on the app. Your continued use of Resolvo after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
