Everything you do all day, in a thumb's reach.

We built the phone app around the five things support people actually do: pick up, answer, check the order, call back, and close.

Swipe to take, resolve or snooze

Swipe a ticket right to take it. Swipe all the way to resolve it, with an Undo if your thumb slips. Swipe left to snooze it until tomorrow morning.

Reply from one slim bar

The reply box stays out of the way until you need it, so you can read the whole conversation. Tap it to write, or tap Resolve right beside it.

Saved replies above the keyboard

Your best answers sit as chips. One tap puts "Your refund takes 3 to 5 working days" into the box. Edit it, send it.

The customer's order, right there

Connect your Shopify store and each ticket shows the order: what's in it, whether it shipped, and the tracking link. One tap adds it to your reply.

Call or WhatsApp in one tap

When a message needs a voice, call the customer or open a WhatsApp chat with them straight from the ticket or their profile.

One queue, most urgent first

Open the app and see only what needs you now, sorted by who has waited longest. Pull down to refresh. Newsletters and automated mail stay out of the way.

Install it in a minute.

The app is in beta and isn't on Google Play yet, so you install it straight from this page.

Download the file

Tap Download for Android on your phone. It's a 3 MB file called Resolvo-Android-1.1.0.apk.

Open it and allow the install

Open the file when it finishes. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or Files app just this once, then tap Install.

Sign in as usual

Open Resolvo and sign in with the email and password you already use. Your tickets are there.

Why does Android show a warning? Android warns about any app that doesn't come from Google Play. This file is signed by Resolvo Group, it asks only for internet access, and nothing is installed beyond the app. The warning goes away once we're on Google Play.

Questions people ask.

Does the app cost extra?

No. It comes with your Resolvo workspace on every plan, including the free one.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not a download yet. On iPhone, open app.resolvogroup.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen with its own icon.

Does it work without internet?

Not yet. The app needs a connection to load your tickets. Offline drafts are on our list.

Is it safe to install from a website?

The file is signed by Resolvo Group and asks only for internet access. You can compare its fingerprint with the one on this page. We'll also publish on Google Play so you can get it there.

Will it update by itself?

Yes for everything you see on screen. The app opens your live workspace, so new features appear as soon as we release them. Only deeper changes, like notifications, need you to install a new file.

Something looks wrong. Who do I tell?

Write to contact@resolvogroup.com with a screenshot. We read every message during the beta.
