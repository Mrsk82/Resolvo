How to Set Up Online Booking for Your Business in India

Whether you're a doctor, tutor, consultant, or salon owner — getting a public booking link live takes under 10 minutes and costs nothing. Here's the complete guide.

By Resolvo Team · 7 min read · June 2025

Why every service business needs online booking in 2025

In 2025, over 60% of Indian consumers prefer to book appointments online rather than by phone — and that number is higher for anyone under 35. If your business doesn't have an online booking option, you're losing clients to competitors who do.

The good news: setting up online booking no longer requires a website, a developer, or a monthly subscription. It takes 10 minutes and a free account.

Step 1: Choose the right booking tool

For most Indian service professionals, the ideal booking tool has three properties:

  • No app required for clients — a simple web link they can open on any phone
  • Auto-confirmation emails — so clients have a record and can add it to their calendar
  • Free for small volumes — most solo practices don't need to pay for booking software

Resolvo checks all three. Your booking page looks like app.resolvogroup.com/book/your-business-name and works on any device without any app install.

Step 2: Create your account and set up your profile

  1. Go to app.resolvogroup.com/pro and create a free account
  2. Select your category: Doctor, Lawyer, Consultant, Salon/Spa, Tutor/Coach, or Other
  3. Enter your practice or business name — this becomes part of your booking URL
  4. Your booking page is created automatically

💡 Naming tip: Use your actual business name for the URL slug, not a generic term. "dr-priya-sharma-dental" is better than "dental-clinic-mumbai" — it's more memorable and builds your personal brand.

Step 3: Set your availability

Go to Settings → Appointments → Working Hours. Set:

  • Which days you take appointments (Mon–Sat, Mon–Fri, etc.)
  • Start and end time each day
  • Slot duration (15 min, 30 min, 45 min, 60 min, 90 min, 2 hours)
  • Buffer time between appointments (5–15 minutes, so you're not back-to-back constantly)

Default settings work for most professionals: Monday–Saturday, 9am–6pm, 30-minute slots, 15-minute buffer.

Step 4: Share your booking link everywhere

Your booking link is useless until clients know about it. Share it in these places:

  • WhatsApp status — reaches everyone in your contact list passively
  • Instagram bio — put "Book an appointment → [your link]" in your bio
  • Google Business profile — add it as your website or booking URL
  • Email signature — every email you send becomes a subtle CTA
  • Visiting cards — print the short URL on new cards
  • WhatsApp auto-reply — set "Book here: [link]" as your away message

Step 5: Test it before going live

Before sharing the link widely, make a test booking yourself using a different email address. Check:

  • Does the confirmation email arrive? Does it look right?
  • Do the available slots match your working hours?
  • Does the booking appear in your dashboard?

This 5-minute test catches any configuration issues before your clients see them.

What clients experience when they book

Your client opens the link, sees a clean page with your name and available dates. They pick a date, choose a time slot, enter their name and email, describe what they need, and click "Book." They get an email immediately confirming the booking, with the date, time, and a reference number. They get another email 24 hours before as a reminder.

The entire experience from landing on your page to confirmed booking takes under 2 minutes. No account creation, no app download, no phone call required.

Common questions

What if I want to block some days (holidays, leaves)?

You can mark specific dates as unavailable in your dashboard. Clients won't see those dates as available.

What if a client books a slot I need to cancel?

You can cancel any booking from your dashboard. The client gets an automatic email notifying them of the cancellation.

Can I take payment at the time of booking?

Not on the free plan currently. Payment integration is on the roadmap. For now, collect payment in person or via UPI/bank transfer after confirmation.

Final thoughts

Online booking is no longer a "nice to have" — it's the baseline expectation for any service professional. The setup is free, takes under 10 minutes, and starts paying off the first time a client books at midnight without you lifting a finger.
