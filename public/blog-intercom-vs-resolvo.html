Intercom vs Resolvo: Which Support Platform Is Right for Indian Startups?

Intercom is one of the most powerful customer communication platforms in the world. It's also one of the most expensive. Here's an honest look at whether that power is worth it for an Indian startup, and what Resolvo offers instead.

By Resolvo Team · 8 min read · June 2025

What Intercom actually is

Intercom started as a live chat widget for SaaS products and has grown into a full customer engagement platform combining live chat, email support, a help centre, product tours, outbound messaging, and AI. It's excellent software — used by thousands of companies worldwide.

The challenge for Indian startups: Intercom's pricing starts at $39/month (Essential) and the features most teams actually want — multiple inboxes, ticket workflows, custom reports — require the Advanced tier at $99/month or more. At current exchange rates, that's ₹3,300–₹8,300/month before you add seats.

Pricing reality check

⚠️ Intercom pricing gotcha: Intercom charges per seat AND per "resolved conversation" beyond your plan limit. A busy support month can generate unexpected overage charges. Indian startups on a tight budget have been caught off guard by bills 2–3x the base price.

Where Intercom is genuinely better

  • Live chat widget — Intercom's chat bubble is best-in-class, with proactive messaging, in-app product tours, and bot flows
  • Fin AI — Intercom's AI chatbot (Fin) is one of the most capable customer-facing AI tools available. It can answer questions directly from your knowledge base with high accuracy
  • Product-led outreach — if you need to send targeted in-app messages to users based on their behaviour ("user hasn't used feature X in 30 days"), Intercom does this very well
  • Integrations ecosystem — Intercom integrates with 300+ tools

Where Resolvo is better for Indian teams

  • Price — Resolvo is free for small teams. No per-seat, no per-conversation overage surprises
  • All-in-one for Indian service businesses — appointments + support + issue tracking + CRM in one platform. Intercom doesn't handle appointments or issue tracking at all
  • Rupee pricing — no currency conversion surprises when the dollar moves
  • Simpler onboarding — Intercom takes days to configure properly. Resolvo takes hours
  • Email-first support — most Indian customers still use email. Resolvo is built email-first; Intercom is chat-first

Who should use Intercom?

Intercom makes sense if:

  • You're a SaaS product with in-app chat as a primary support channel
  • You need product tours and in-app onboarding sequences
  • You want AI to handle a high volume of FAQ-type chat queries autonomously
  • You have budget: $99+/month is reasonable for your stage
  • Your customers primarily reach you via chat, not email

Who should use Resolvo?

Resolvo makes sense if:

  • Your customers primarily email you for support
  • You're an Indian startup or SMB where dollar pricing is a real concern
  • You need appointments + support + issue tracking in one place
  • You have a small team (1–15 agents) and don't need complex chat bot flows
  • You want to start free and grow into paid features

Final verdict

Intercom is the right choice if you're a well-funded SaaS startup where in-app chat and product-led growth are core to your strategy. Resolvo is the right choice for Indian service businesses, e-commerce support teams, and early-stage startups where email is the main support channel and every rupee counts.

They're solving slightly different problems. Know which problem you have.
