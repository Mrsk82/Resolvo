What Resolvo does for a Shopify store

The order sits next to the conversation, and one tap turns it into a reply.

The order beside the ticket

Connect your store in five minutes

You decide what Resolvo can see, and it can only read.

  1. In Shopify, create a custom app and turn on read_orders and read_customers.
  2. Install the app and copy its Client ID and secret (or its Admin API access token).
  3. In Resolvo open Settings, then Integrations, then Shopify.
  4. Enter your store address (your-store.myshopify.com) and paste the keys, then press Test.
  5. Open any ticket from a customer who ordered: their order appears beside it.

What it does not do

So you know before you start.

  • It is read-only. Refunds, cancellations and edits stay in Shopify.
  • It does not send WhatsApp promotions. Business-started WhatsApp messages need templates approved by WhatsApp: free template generator.
  • It does not replace your Shopify admin. It saves the trips there.
