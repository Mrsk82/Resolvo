How the numbers are worked out

  • Load is the hours of work arriving each day divided by the hours your team has: messages x minutes each, divided by people x support hours.
  • Wait uses the Erlang C queueing formula, the standard way call centres estimate how long people wait. It assumes messages arrive evenly and are handled in order.
  • Comfortable load means about 75 percent of support time used, which leaves room for busy hours and for people to take a break.
  • Costs are list prices. Freshdesk Growth is $19 per agent, Zendesk Suite Team is $55, and SachDesk Starter is ₹999. Resolvo Pro is ₹999 per brand, whatever the team size.
This is an estimate to help you decide, not a promise. If your result says your team is stretched, the fix is usually a mix of canned replies, keeping newsletters out of the queue, and one more pair of hands.

Questions people ask

What does 'load' mean?

It is how much of your team's available support time is used by incoming messages. Above about 80 percent, replies start to slip. Above 100 percent, a backlog builds every day.

Why is the wait time only an estimate?

It assumes messages arrive evenly through the day and are handled in order. Real days have rushes, so your actual wait at busy hours will be longer.

Are the competitor prices current?

They are list prices we found in September 2026, billed annually and before tax. Please confirm on the vendors' pages.
