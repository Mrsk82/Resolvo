Resolvo vs SachDesk

Two new helpdesks for Indian teams. Which one fits yours?

SachDesk (Samvaads) is a big all-in-one workspace. Resolvo is a focused helpdesk with flat pricing per brand. Here is where each one is stronger.

Try Resolvo free for 14 days See a demo

The short version

Choose SachDesk if you

  • need Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, SMS or voice in the same inbox
  • want marketing campaigns, journeys and lead sequences in the same tool
  • want AI agents that answer customers on their own
  • have up to 3 agents and want a free plan with WhatsApp

Choose Resolvo if you

  • have a small team of 3 or more and want one flat price, not a price per agent
  • want a simpler helpdesk with SLA timers, a shared inbox and a customer record, without extra modules to learn
  • want AI to draft replies that a person approves before anything is sent
  • want engineers and support in one place, with an issue tracker, Kanban and sprints
  • want a calm inbox: Focus mode for one ticket at a time, newsletters and alerts kept out of the queue, and plain-language insights

What a 5-agent team pays

SachDesk Starter: 5 agents × ₹999 = ₹4,995 per month, billed annually, plus 18% GST. Starter adds Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, Slack and Teams over the free plan.

Resolvo Pro: ₹999 per month for the brand, however many agents you add.

For 1 to 3 agents who only need the basics, SachDesk's free plan costs nothing. For larger teams the per-agent price adds up quickly.
