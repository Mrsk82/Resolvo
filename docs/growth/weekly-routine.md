# Weekly growth routine (30 minutes, Monday)

1. **Read the Monday email** (or open Owner Console, Growth tab, last 7 days). Note visits, sign-ups, demo and design-partner requests, messages.
2. **Check the funnel.** If people sign up but do not connect email or WhatsApp, the set-up is the problem, not the traffic. Message the people listed under "Stuck" personally, today.
3. **Look at sources.** Which source gave sign-ups, not just visits? Do more of that, drop what gave nothing after four weeks.
4. **Pick one thing to publish this week:** a LinkedIn post (`linkedin-posts.md`), one blog article, or a tool share. Tag every link.
5. **Reply to every lead within one working day.** Demo requests and design-partner applications are in the Growth tab and in your inbox.
6. **Search Console, monthly:** pages with impressions but few clicks need a better title or description.
7. **Write down one decision** (what you will do differently next week). Keep it in your notes.

Do not send cold email in bulk. The prepared 50-lead pack stays unsent until you decide, and uses a tagged link so replies and visits are attributed.
