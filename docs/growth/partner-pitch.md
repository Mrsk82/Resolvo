# Partner and referrer pitch

For accountants, e-commerce agencies, Shopify freelancers and WhatsApp business-API providers who already talk to small brands. Be clear that nothing below is a promise of volume. Agree the referral terms in writing before anyone is paid; no commission is offered by default.

## Short message (WhatsApp or email)
Hi <name>, I run Resolvo, a support inbox for Indian brands that handle customers over email and WhatsApp (shared inbox, response-time tracking, a phone app, flat price per brand). Some of your clients probably have this problem. If you would like to try it first so you can judge if it fits them, I will set you up free and help with the first workspace. If it fits, a tagged link makes it easy to see who came from you: [LINK: source=<partner-name>, medium=referral, campaign=partner]

## What a partner gets
- A free workspace to try and show clients.
- A personal link so every sign-up from them is visible in the Growth tab under their name.
- Help with set-up for their first client.

## What to ask in return
- An honest opinion on what is missing.
- Introductions only where it is a genuine fit.

## Terms to decide before you say yes (your call)
- Whether there is a referral fee, how much, for how long, and how it is paid.
- Who owns the client relationship and support for the client's workspace.

## Tracking
Use `utm_source=<partner-name>`, `utm_medium=referral`, `utm_campaign=partner`. Sign-ups and demo requests appear in Growth under that source label.
