## 1. The problem
Most small brands run support from a Gmail inbox and a WhatsApp phone passed between three people.

You know it is broken when:
- two people reply to the same customer
- a message sits unanswered over a weekend and nobody noticed
- you cannot say how fast you actually reply

We built Resolvo for that stage: one shared inbox for email and WhatsApp, a response-time target on every ticket, and a flat price per brand, not per agent.
