## Long description
Resolvo is customer support software built for small Indian brands that run support on Gmail and WhatsApp.

Every email and WhatsApp message lands in one shared inbox. Each becomes a ticket with an owner, a priority and a first-response target, so nothing is forgotten and nobody replies twice. AI drafts suggest replies that your team reviews before sending. Shopify order details can be looked up inside a ticket. A phone app (Android) lets you answer on the move.

Pricing is per brand, not per agent: Free at ₹0, Pro at ₹999 and Enterprise at ₹2,499 per month. A 14-day trial of everything is included. Check the pricing page for what each plan includes.
